ALPHA 1.0.15 — METROLOGY ROLLOUT & CALIBRATION

Artifact version: 1.0.15-alpha
Minecraft: 1.21.1
NeoForge: 21.1.249
Java: 21

PURPOSE
Promote Alpha 1.0.14 metrology from a Tank Level Sensor demonstration into shared measurement infrastructure used across redstone, electrical, pneumatic and mechatronic instruments.

DELIVERED
- MetrologySupport shared sampling, snapshots, bounded conditioning, Engineering Port quality projection and diagnostic formatting.
- Engineering Light Sensor: conditioned repeated measurements with bias/noise/drift/age/uncertainty metadata.
- Entity Density Sensor: preserves the physical count before clamping so >15 occupancy is explicitly SATURATED.
- Tank Level Sensor: migrated to shared metrology support while preserving its existing static measurement API.
- Servo Position Sensor: compares conditioned feedback against authoritative servo position.
- Copper Circuit Meter: scheduled non-invasive voltage sampling independent of Jade/UI polling.
- Pneumatic Flow Meter: scheduled 0..100 flow-proxy measurement with explicit range saturation.
- Calibration Module: BACK observed input + LEFT known reference + FRONT calibrated output, with live residual and uncertainty diagnostics while retaining all five historical transfer profiles.
- Measurement quality is projected to the Engineering Port contract only where an equivalent state exists; DEGRADED remains diagnostic metadata rather than being mislabeled as FAULT.
- Runtime metrology remains level-scoped transient state and never expands BlockState cardinality.

HARD INVARIANTS
1. World-facing redstone signals remain bounded to 0..15.
2. Measurement uncertainty proxy is diagnostic metadata, not formal GUM expanded uncertainty and not interchangeable with signed measurement error/bias.
3. UI/Jade/render polling must never determine sample cadence or authoritative physics state.
4. Scheduled instrument sampling owns metrology history for copper and pneumatic meters.
5. GeckoLib remains display-only and cannot determine simulation state.
