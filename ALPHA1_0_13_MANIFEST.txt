Mechatronics visualization completion carried into the Alpha 1.0.14 promotion branch:
- GeckoLib 4.9.2 now renders Servo Actuator, Pneumatic Cylinder, and Pneumatic Proportional Valve through a shared MechatronicsVisualBlockEntity.
- Servo simulation position maps to shaft rotation; applied velocity controls the rate of authoritative position change; brake freezes motion in physics before the snapshot reaches rendering.
- Pneumatic Cylinder pressure/position maps to piston-rod extension and a pressure indicator.
- Proportional Valve opening 0..15 maps to valve-spool position.
- RuntimeIntStore.peek() returns cloned read-only runtime snapshots and never creates/resizes simulation state.
- MechatronicsVisualState is immutable and renderer-facing.
- HARD INVARIANT: GeckoLib displays simulation state only. Rendering, FPS, model bones, and animation lifecycle never write physics state.
