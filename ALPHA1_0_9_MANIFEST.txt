Required platform dependencies:
- JEI 19.27.0.336 — required CLIENT
- Jade 15.10.6 — required BOTH
- GeckoLib 4.9.2 — required BOTH
- Cloth Config 15.0.140 — required CLIENT
- Fusion 1.3.14 — required CLIENT (Maven artifact: 1.3.14-neoforge-mc1.21.1)

Architecture decision:
Alpha 1.0.9 intentionally promotes these libraries from optional/candidate status into the RSE platform contract. Missing dependencies must prevent startup on the declared side. The dependencies are installed normally and are not shaded into the RSE jar.

Engineering purpose:
JEI supports progression and recipe information; Jade supports engineering HUD/data diagnostics; GeckoLib supports articulated machine animation; Cloth Config supports maintainable configuration screens; Fusion supports connected and advanced engineering visuals.
