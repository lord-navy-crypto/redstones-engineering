Contracts:
- Acceptance history is created only by an explicit player action: Shift + right-click the PID Controller FRONT face.
- Ordinary right-click keeps tuning-preset cycling; Shift + right-click on non-FRONT faces keeps the established PID runtime-reset action.
- Each record freezes the existing authoritative EngineeringAcceptanceSnapshot plus game tick, tuning preset, and a monotonic local sequence number.
- A controller retains at most 8 records and a level retains at most 256 controller timelines; history cannot grow without bound.
- Run history lives outside BlockState and outside PID RuntimeIntStore. It is diagnostic runtime evidence and is intentionally transient in Alpha 1.0.20.
- Latest-versus-previous comparison reports commissioning-score delta, topology-issue delta, and IMPROVED/SAME/REGRESSED/INCOMPARABLE trend.
- Jade may read and present captured history, but HUD polling never creates records and never becomes sampling, simulation, topology, or controller authority.
- Vanilla-compatible world-facing redstone remains bounded to 0..15.
