RSE Alpha 1.0.7 — Legacy Wiring & Port Diagnostics
Version: 1.0.7-alpha
License: MIT

Renovates early wiring rather than adding another machine layer.
- Instrument Cable becomes a six-direction multi-drop physical bus.
- InstrumentNetwork follows actual connected cable edges.
- Probe sensing face and instrumentation connection face are separated.
- Insulated Redstone, Copper, Lapis and Quartz expose exact port directions.
- Direct contact between incompatible transmission media reports DOMAIN_MISMATCH.
- Vanilla-facing redstone remains bounded to 0..15.
- Alpha 1.0.6 verifier is forward-compatible so historical contracts survive future milestones.
