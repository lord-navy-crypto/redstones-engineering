Milestone: Engineering UX & Topology Visualization

Contracts:
- Topology visualization is a read-only projection of EngineeringPortProvider descriptors, live EngineeringPortSnapshot observations, and PortCompatibility rules.
- Visualization never becomes a second topology solver and never writes BlockState, RuntimeIntStore, network registries, controller state, or machine physics.
- Each physical face is classified as CONNECTED, OPEN, ISOLATED, DOMAIN_MISMATCH, DIRECTION_MISMATCH, or UNLOADED.
- The Jade Engineering HUD preserves targeted-face detail and adds an all-face topology summary with domain, direction, link status, and live quality where available.
- Immediate neighbor classification uses the existing Engineering Port contract; transmission/network ownership remains in the existing simulation and topology code.
- Vanilla-compatible world-facing redstone remains bounded to 0..15.
