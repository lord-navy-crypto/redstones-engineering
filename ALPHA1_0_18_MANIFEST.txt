Milestone: Engineering Acceptance & Traceability

Contracts:
- Engineering acceptance consumes immutable topology and commissioning snapshots; it never samples or mutates authoritative simulation state directly.
- Verdicts are explicit: NOT_READY, PASS, MARGINAL, or FAIL.
- Known Engineering Port domain/direction mismatches fail acceptance even if closed-loop commissioning otherwise passes.
- Unavailable, idle, or running commissioning evidence remains NOT_READY when topology is structurally healthy.
- MARGINAL and FAIL commissioning states preserve their severity without redefining the established commissioning score.
- Trace output carries deterministic topology counts, commissioning state/score, acceptance status, and stable issue codes.
- OPEN, ISOLATED, and UNLOADED faces remain observational states rather than automatic failures; Alpha 1.0.17 topologyIssue semantics remain authoritative.
- Vanilla-compatible world-facing redstone remains bounded to 0..15.
- UI, Jade, rendering, and diagnostics remain downstream observers and never gain authority over physics, topology, sampling, or control.
