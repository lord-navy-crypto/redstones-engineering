Milestone: Metrology & Uncertainty

Engineering objective:
- Upgrade RSE sensors from value-only devices into measurement systems that report how trustworthy a reading is.
- Preserve the vanilla-compatible 0..15 world-facing signal boundary while keeping richer measurement metadata in transient engineering runtime state.
- Keep measurement error concepts separate: bias is reported explicitly; uncertainty is a diagnostic proxy rather than a claim of formal GUM expanded uncertainty.

Metrology core:
- MeasurementSnapshot: immutable reading + Repeatability + bias + drift + noise + resolution + saturation + sample age + sample count + uncertainty proxy + quality.
- MeasurementQuality: GOOD, DEGRADED, SATURATED, STALE, INVALID.
- MetrologyTracker: 32-sample rolling residual window with repeatability, first-difference noise, half-window drift, bias and conservative RSS uncertainty-proxy estimates.
- MetrologyStore: weak level-scoped transient storage that keeps high-cardinality diagnostics out of BlockState.

Sensor integration:
- Tank Level Sensor now conditions its physical reading through the existing SensorModel before publishing a 0..15 redstone output.
- The same sample feeds metrology diagnostics without changing the vanilla-facing signal contract.
- Fluid columns beyond the 0..15 representable range explicitly report SATURATED rather than silently pretending the clipped reading is exact.
- Readout exposes repeatability, bias, drift, noise, resolution, sample age, samples, uncertainty proxy and quality.

Alpha 1.0.13 visualization completion included in this promotion:
- GeckoLib visual block entity, model, renderer and client registration for Servo Actuator, Pneumatic Cylinder and Proportional Valve.
- Immutable simulation-to-render snapshots and RuntimeIntStore.peek() enforce one-way data flow.
- Servo shaft rotation, pneumatic rod extension, valve spool travel and pressure indicators are derived only from simulation state.

Verification contracts:
- Stable reference-aligned measurements classify GOOD.
- Bias/drift produce DEGRADED quality and nontrivial uncertainty proxy.
- Saturation and stale sample age are explicit quality states.
- Visualization projection remains normalized and read-only.
- Previous topology, copper, dependency and engineering regression gates remain required.

HARD INVARIANTS:
1. GeckoLib may display simulation state but must never determine physics state.
2. World-facing engineering signals remain bounded to vanilla-compatible 0..15 unless a domain explicitly defines an internal richer representation.
3. Measurement uncertainty proxy is diagnostic metadata; it is not interchangeable with measurement error or formal expanded uncertainty.
